How it works

It meets the child where they are, then moves.

There is no level select and nothing to unlock. BrainPlay watches how a round actually goes and quietly changes what it asks next.

🎚️

Difficulty that follows the child

Every game holds five age bands. A rolling average of how the child is doing shifts the band up or down, so the work stays just hard enough — never a wall, never a shrug.

⭐

Stars, not scores

A round ends in one, two or three stars. Children never see a point total, because a number invites comparison and a star invites another go.

🎯

A daily quest

A small handful of games chosen for that child each day — a reason to come back that isn't a streak counter or a nag.

🕹️

Remote only, by design

Every board is reachable with the D-pad and OK. Big targets, clear focus, nothing that needs a mouse, a keyboard or a steady finger.

🔇

Gentle by default

Calm mode softens motion and sound, relaxed pace gives longer to answer, and read-aloud speaks the prompt for children who aren't reading yet.

🛡️

Nothing to buy, nobody to talk to

No advertising, no in-app purchases, no chat, no social features. Just the games.

The worlds

For grown-ups

A quiet room behind a PIN.

Progress per child, a daily screen-time limit, the gentle-mode switches and profile management all live behind a four-digit PIN, out of the way of the child and out of the way of play.

👦

A profile per child

Each child keeps their own age, stars, badges and difficulty — several children share one television without sharing a score.

⏳

Screen-time limit

Set how long a day's play lasts. The app counts only time a game was actually on screen.

📈

See what they're practising

Mastery per world and per game, so you can tell the difference between "played a lot" and "getting better".
