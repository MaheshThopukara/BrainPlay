Tic Tac Toe

Ages 3–13 3 min Easy

Classic Tic Tac Toe vs the computer. Place your X to make a row, column, or diagonal of three.

Builds

  • ❌ Strategy
  • 🧠 Look ahead
  • 🎯 Pattern building
  • 🤖 Vs computer

How to play

  1. D-pad to a cell
  2. Press OK to place X
  3. Block the computer
  4. Make 3 in a row
  5. Three in a row wins the round
Did you know? Tic-Tac-Toe was played in ancient Egypt over 3000 years ago.

Brick Breaker

Ages 3–13 3 min Medium

Bounce the ball off your paddle to smash every brick on the screen.

Builds

  • 🧱 Aim + timing
  • ⚡ Hand-eye coord
  • 🎯 Trajectory sense
  • 🧠 Quick math

How to play

  1. D-pad to move paddle
  2. Keep ball in play
  3. Aim at the bricks
  4. Break every one
  5. Clear the whole wall of bricks
Did you know? Brick Breaker was inspired by Atari's 1976 Breakout.

Block Stack

Ages 3–13 4 min Hard

Rotate and drop the falling blocks to make full rows. Keep going as long as you can —

Builds

  • 🟦 Spatial reasoning
  • ⚡ Fast decisions
  • 🧠 Pattern packing
  • 🎯 Precision drops

How to play

  1. Left / right to move
  2. OK to rotate
  3. Down to drop faster
  4. Up to slam down
  5. A full row clears and scores
Did you know? Falling-block puzzles were invented back in 1984.

Dino Dash

Ages 3–13 2 min Medium

Your dino runs on its own and the ground speeds up. Jump over the rocks and duck under the birds — see how far you can run!

Builds

  • ⚡ Quick reactions
  • ⏱️ Timing
  • 🎯 Focus under speed
  • 🦖 Never-give-up spirit

How to play

  1. The dino runs by itself
  2. Press OK or Up to jump over rocks
  3. Hold Down to duck under birds
  4. A bump costs a heart — you have a few
  5. Win all 3 stars and the spare hearts go — the next bump ends the run!
Did you know? It's an “endless runner” — the track never stops, so it's you vs. your best distance.

Connect Four

Ages 3–13 5 min Medium

Drop your coins down the columns. First to line up four — across, down or on a slant — wins. Play a friend on the sofa, or take on the computer.

Builds

  • 🔴 Plan ahead
  • 🛡️ Spot and block
  • 🧠 Think in lines
  • 🤝 Play together

How to play

  1. Pick two players or the computer
  2. ◀ ▶ to choose a column
  3. OK drops your coin to the bottom
  4. Line up four in any direction
  5. Block them before they get four!
Did you know? A slanted four counts just as much as a straight one.
