Cup Shuffle

Ages 3–13 3 min Easy

Watch which cup hides the ball, then keep your eyes on it as the cups shuffle. Pick the right cup when they stop.

Builds

  • 👀 Visual tracking
  • 🧠 Working memory
  • 🎯 Sustained attention
  • 🧘 Patience and focus

How to play

  1. Watch the ball hide under a cup
  2. Track that cup as they shuffle
  3. D-pad to pick a cup
  4. Press OK to lift
  5. Right cup, more ⭐!

Simon's Orders

Ages 3–13 3 min Medium

A voice calls out a move — Jump, Duck, Lean left, Lean right — and you do it with the D-pad arrow. But ONLY when it starts with “Simon says”! If Simon didn't say it, freeze and don't press.

Builds

  • 🧠 Impulse control
  • 👂 Careful listening
  • ⚡ Quick reactions
  • 🎯 Focus

How to play

  1. Listen and watch for the command
  2. ▲ Jump · ▼ Duck · ◀ Lean left · ▶ Lean right
  3. Press the arrow ONLY if it said “Simon says”
  4. No “Simon says”? Freeze — don't press!
  5. Fewer slips = more ⭐!
Did you know? Stopping yourself from acting is called impulse control — a real superpower for your brain.
