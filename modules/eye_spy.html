Color Mix

Ages 3–10 3 min Easy

Two paints get mixed — pick the colour they make. Red, yellow and blue are the three that make all the rest.

Builds

  • 🎨 Colour mixing
  • 🧠 Colour theory
  • 👀 Visual prediction
  • 🌈 Art skill

How to play

  1. See the two colours
  2. Imagine them blended
  3. D-pad to pick result
  4. Press OK to lock
  5. Right blend wins stars!
Did you know? Red + yellow = orange. Yellow + blue = green!

Direction Spy

Ages 3–8 3 min Easy

A car, a dog, a fish — press the way it is going. Keep up: the faster you spot it, the more you catch.

Builds

  • 🚗 Spot the facing
  • ⚡ Quick reaction
  • 🧭 Up, down, left, right
  • 🎯 Fast judgement

How to play

  1. An object appears in the middle
  2. Look which way it's going
  3. Press that way on the remote
  4. Some can go up and down too
  5. Quick and right wins stars!
Did you know? Fish swim the way their nose points.

Color Bands

Colour swatches appear — sort them lightest to darkest.

Silhouette Match

Ages 3–13 3 min Medium

A tower of blocks stands in front of you. Turn it around, then find the flat picture of it seen from the top, the front or the side.

Builds

  • 🧊 See in 3D
  • 📐 Read plans
  • 👁️ Spot hidden blocks
  • 🧠 Space sense

How to play

  1. Look at the tower of blocks
  2. Press UP, then ◀ ▶ to turn it
  3. Read which view is asked for
  4. Pick the flat picture that matches
  5. Right view = more ⭐!
Did you know? Builders read three flat views before they build anything.

Rotate Vision

Ages 3–13 3 min Hard

A picture shows up, then a few more just like it. One is the same picture turned to a new angle — the rest are flipped the wrong way. Pick the turned one!

Builds

  • 🔄 Mental rotation
  • 🧠 Spatial logic
  • 🪞 Spot the flip
  • 👀 Careful looking

How to play

  1. Look at the picture on top
  2. Check each choice below
  3. Turn it around in your head
  4. Press OK on the one that is just spun
  5. Skip the flipped ones!
Did you know? A turned picture keeps the same side out; a flip swaps it.

Snapshot

Ages 3–13 3 min Easy

Take a photo with your eyes! A few pictures show for a moment, then hide. Pick the one that was really in your photo.

Builds

  • 📸 Visual memory
  • 🧠 Remembering
  • 👀 Looking closely
  • 🎯 Sharp focus

How to play

  1. Look hard while the photo shows
  2. Try to remember every picture
  3. Watch the photo hide away
  4. Pick the one that was in it
  5. Get them right to win stars!
Did you know? Your brain takes a 'snapshot' every time you blink.

Glow Grid

Ages 3–13 3 min Medium

Some tiles light up for a moment, then go dark. Now tap them back — move the cursor to each tile you remember and press OK. Reproduce the whole pattern to win!

Builds

  • 🧠 Working memory
  • 📍 Spatial memory
  • 👀 Looking closely
  • 🎯 Focus & recall

How to play

  1. Watch which tiles glow
  2. The lights turn off — remember where they were
  3. D-pad to a tile, press OK to light it back
  4. A wrong tap just costs a star — keep going
  5. Tap the whole pattern back for more ⭐!
Did you know? Remembering where things are is called spatial memory — you use it to find your shoes!

  3. Press OK on each item

  5. Find them all to win stars!
Did you know? Hidden-object puzzles have been around since the 1800s.
