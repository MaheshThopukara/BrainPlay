Number Match

Ages 3–8 3 min Easy

Match each number to the group with that many — a numeral and a quantity, joined. One side shows numerals, the other shows dots; connect the pairs.

Builds

  • 🔢 Number-quantity link
  • 🧠 Symbol recognition
  • 🎯 Matching strategy
  • ⭐ Cardinality sense

How to play · TV remote· touch

  1. The glowing card on the left is your target
  2. ◀ ▶ move across the groups on the right
  3. OK picks the group with that many
  4. A green line joins a correct pair
  5. Match every pair for ⭐!
  1. The glowing card on the left is your target
  2. Tap the group with that many
  3. A green line joins a correct pair
  4. Match every pair for ⭐!
Did you know? Matching a numeral to its quantity is the very first step in number sense.

  3. D-pad to a number, press OK to pick
  4. Closest guess wins — more ⭐!

A balance scale weighs up two amounts — dot piles at first, then numbers, decimals, fractions and sums to work out. Spot the bigger side.

Builds

  • 🧠 Ordering numbers

How to play · TV remote· touch

  2. Figure out each side, then press ◀ or ▶ toward the bigger one
  3. The beam tips and the > < = sign drops into the middle
  4. Same on both sides? Press OK for equal
  5. Get it right for more ⭐!

Add It Up

Ages 5–13 4 min Medium

Add the columns like on paper. Start at the ones on the right, and when a column makes ten or more, carry the ten up into the next box.

Builds

  • 🪜 Column adding
  • ⬆️ Carrying
  • 🔢 Place value at work
  • ⭐ Sums on paper

How to play · TV remote· touch

  1. Start at the ones — the column on the right
  2. Press ◀ or ▶ to pick a digit, then OK to drop it in
  3. Ten or more? Write the ones digit, carry the ten into the box above
  4. Nothing to carry? That box is a 0 — it still needs filling
  5. On the hardest level the answer is given and a digit is missing — work backwards!

  3. Give every plate the SAME amount — go round and round
  4. Stop when too few are left to give everyone one more
  5. Press OK to Share it! — a fair share earns more ⭐!
