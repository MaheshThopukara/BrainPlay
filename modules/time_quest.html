Time Quest

Clocks, calendars and routines. Practice reading time, ordering the day, and reasoning about before / after.

13 games · in the order we'd teach them

🌅

Morning · Noon · Night

Ages 3–8 3 min Easy

Breakfast, lunch, bedtime — pick which part of the day each scene belongs to.

Builds

  • 🌅 Time of day
  • 🧠 Daily rhythm
  • 📅 Time sense
  • 🎯 Quick sort

How to play

  1. Read the question — or look at the picture
  2. Think when in the day it happens
  3. ▲ ▼ move down the choices
  4. Press OK on the time of day
  5. Green card = right — more ⭐!
Did you know? Roosters crow at morning — they're nature's alarm clock.

Faster or Slower

Ages 3–10 3 min Easy

A snail, a cheetah, a rocket — read the question and pick the one that moves faster. Some questions ask for the SLOWER one instead, so read carefully!

Builds

  • ⚡ Speed sense
  • 🧠 Compare and judge
  • 🐆 Animal & vehicle facts
  • 🎯 Careful reading

How to play

  1. Read the question — faster, or slower?
  2. Look at every choice on the card
  3. ▲ ▼ move down the choices
  4. Press OK on your pick
  5. Green card = right — more ⭐!
Did you know? A cheetah runs about 120 km an hour — the fastest animal on land.

Tomorrow & Yesterday

Ages 3–10 3 min Easy

What is the day after Monday? What day is three days before Friday? Work along the week and pick the right day.

Builds

  • 🔁 Day relations
  • 📅 Week order
  • 🧠 Sequence sense
  • ⏳ Before and after

How to play

  1. Read the day the question names
  2. Spot BEFORE or AFTER — they go opposite ways
  3. Count that many days along the week
  4. Press OK on the day you land on
  5. Right day = more ⭐!
Did you know? Saturday always follows Friday — the week never changes its order.
